PETS studies how to allocate a fixed test-time reasoning budget across a set of questions. Rather than sampling every question uniformly, it estimates difficulty and spends additional trajectories where they are expected to improve the final majority-vote outcome.

The framework includes an offline allocation method based on a Bayesian Markov decision process and an Optimistic Knowledge Gradient policy, as well as a lightweight online estimator that begins with a four-sample warm-up. Across Qwen and GPT-OSS models on GPQA, AIME, HMMT, and BRUMO, PETS reduced sampling budgets by up to 75% offline and 55% online versus uniform allocation.
